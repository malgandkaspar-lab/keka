Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 September 2026

This policy explains how Shorts Factory ("the app") handles information, including data obtained from Google through YouTube API Services. The app is a self-hosted tool operated by the owner of the Fliqo YouTube channel ("we"). Contact: fliqotv@gmail.com.

1. YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By connecting a YouTube channel you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

2. What Google user data we access

When the channel owner connects a YouTube channel with Google sign-in, the app requests these permissions:

  • youtube.upload - upload videos and thumbnails to the connected channel and set their privacy status or scheduled publish time.
  • youtube.readonly - read the connected channel's name, ID and thumbnail, and the status and public statistics of its own videos.
  • yt-analytics.readonly - read YouTube Analytics reports (for example views, watch time and average view percentage) for the channel's own videos.

The app does not access comments, subscribers' personal information, private messages, other channels or any other Google service.

3. How we use it

  • To upload and schedule the channel's videos, and to show their upload status in the app.
  • To show the channel owner analytics for their videos in the app.
  • To learn which formats perform best. The app ranks its own settings (topic category, hook style and video length) by performance. Only the names of the best-ranked settings, for example "space", "question" or "30 seconds", may be included in instructions sent to the AI text service that writes new scripts. No views, video IDs, channel information, account data or tokens are sent.

We do not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or transfer it to anyone except as described here. Google user data is not used to develop, improve or train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.

Shorts Factory's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Storage and security

  • All data is stored in the app's own database, which runs on the channel owner's computer. It is not stored on third-party servers.
  • Google OAuth access and refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM and are never shown in the app or written to logs.
  • Access to the app requires a password-protected account.

5. Other services the app uses

To make videos, the app sends content (not Google user data, except the setting names described in section 3) to these services: Anthropic (AI text generation and web research), Pexels (stock footage search), and Wikipedia (research). Voice, subtitles and video rendering run locally on the owner's computer.

6. Retention and deletion

  • Tokens and channel information are kept only while the channel is connected. Choosing Disconnect in the app deletes them and revokes the app's access at Google.
  • Analytics snapshots are kept for as long as the related video exists in the app and are deleted together with the video.
  • Stored YouTube API data is refreshed or deleted at least every 30 days, as required by the YouTube API Services Developer Policies.
  • You can also revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account: security.google.com/settings/security/permissions.
  • To request deletion of any data, email fliqotv@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days.

7. Cookies

The app uses one session cookie to keep the owner signed in and a short-lived cookie to protect the Google sign-in flow. It uses no tracking or advertising cookies. This website uses no cookies.

8. Changes

We will update the effective date above when this policy changes.
